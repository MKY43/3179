Recent Major Cyclones

Major Category 4/5 cyclones (2006-2015) frequently struck northern Australia , influenced by the region's tropical conditions.

These intense cyclones often form in warm tropical waters, highlighting the vulnerability of northern coastal regions.

The most destructive cyclone was Yasi with a radius of 500km, roughly the distance from Melbourne to Canberra

Cyclone Dynamics

It can be observed that lower central pressure often leads to higher wind speeds, illustrating the intensity changes across cyclone timelines

Cyclone wind speeds vary significantly, with 17-24 m/s being the least frequent range across all events.

Impact Of Cyclones

Cyclone Yasi caused the most damage and affected the most people, yet had only one recorded fatality

Despite their smaller impact, Cyclones George and Ingrid had the highest fatality count, with five deaths each

Cyclones Over The Years

Cyclone occurrences from 1970 to 2024 show a slight downward trend, with most being non-severe (< Category 4)

Severe cyclones (Category 4+) remain less frequent than non-severe as annual cyclone counts gradually decrease
